What is real in this lab

  
Each board runs the actual RakshaWave firmware (firmware/esp32/main.ino, unmodified), compiled to WebAssembly. Every chip around it is emulated from its datasheet at register level, and the boards talk to each other only through a physical radio channel.

  

    
PartHow it is emulated

    
ESP32-C3 + firmwareReal firmware and the same library APIs (sandeepmistry LoRa, Adafruit MPU6050/SSD1306/GFX, TinyGPSPlus, ArduinoJson 6). Time is the chip's own: I²C at 100/400 kHz, SPI at 8 MHz, UARTs at their baud rates, delay(), and blocking LoRa transmissions all take their real duration.

    
SX1278 (Ra-01)Register map, FIFO, operating modes, RX single timeout, IRQ flags, packet RSSI/SNR/frequency-error registers, crystal tolerance (±10 ppm), PA_BOOST output power and supply current. Time on air from Semtech AN1200.13.

    
433 MHz channelExact two-ray ground reflection (highway) or log-distance (n = 2.9 / 3.5), correlated log-normal shadowing, Rician fading per frame, kTB + 7 dB noise figure, LoRa SNR limits per spreading factor, 6 dB co-channel capture, collisions, half-duplex, 5 dB in-cabin body loss.

    
MPU-6050Registers, reset, full-scale ranges, 16-bit saturation, digital low-pass filter, noise density, offsets. Driven by a 7-DOF car model (heave, pitch, roll, four wheels, tyre lift-off) over an ISO 8608 road with potholes and speed bumps; crashes use a crash-test deceleration pulse.

    
SSD1306 OLEDThe picture is rebuilt from the I²C byte stream: commands, addressing modes, remap, charge pump, contrast.

    
GPSNMEA GGA/GSA/RMC/VTG at 9600 baud, 1 Hz, real checksums, 2.5 m CEP error, time to first fix.

    
PowerPer-part supply currents from datasheets, TPS63001 efficiency, LiFePO4 cell (the MCP73123 charger is a LiFePO4 charger).

  

  
Not emulated: the Raspberry Pi (camera, Python stack, push-to-talk audio). Its USB serial lines can be typed in the Serial tab. The PCB is not routed yet, so where parts sit on the 3D HAT is a plausible arrangement.
